JOB TITLE: AI/ML Engineer

COMPANY: Tech Solutions India Pvt Ltd

LOCATION: Bangalore / Remote

EXPERIENCE: 1-3 years

JOB DESCRIPTION:

We are seeking a passionate AI/ML Engineer to join our growing team. The ideal candidate will work on developing and deploying machine learning models for real-world applications.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Design, develop, and deploy machine learning models for various business applications
- Work with large datasets to extract insights and build predictive models
- Implement Natural Language Processing (NLP) solutions for text analytics
- Develop and maintain ML pipelines and workflows
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate AI solutions
- Optimize model performance and scalability
- Create and maintain technical documentation

REQUIRED SKILLS:
- Python programming with strong fundamentals
- Machine Learning frameworks: Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Keras
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) experience
- Experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI
- Understanding of deep learning architectures
- SQL and database management
- Git and version control
- Data preprocessing and feature engineering
- Model evaluation and validation techniques

PREFERRED SKILLS:
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Knowledge of MLOps and model deployment
- Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes
- Experience with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems
- LangChain, Hugging Face, or similar frameworks
- Vector databases (ChromaDB, Pinecone, Weaviate)
- FastAPI or Flask for API development
- Experience with Streamlit or Gradio for UI development
- Understanding of transformer architectures
- Computer vision experience is a plus

EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
- Online certifications in AI/ML are highly valued

SOFT SKILLS:
- Strong problem-solving abilities
- Good communication skills
- Team collaboration
- Ability to work independently
- Continuous learning mindset

WHAT WE OFFER:
- Competitive salary
- Work from home flexibility
- Learning and development opportunities
- Health insurance
- Collaborative work environment
