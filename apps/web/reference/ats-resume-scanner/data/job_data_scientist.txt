JOB TITLE: Data Scientist

COMPANY: DataTech Analytics India

LOCATION: Mumbai / Hybrid

EXPERIENCE: 2-4 years

JOB DESCRIPTION:

We are looking for an analytical and detail-oriented Data Scientist to join our analytics team. You will be responsible for analyzing complex datasets, building predictive models, and delivering actionable insights to drive business decisions.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Analyze large, complex datasets to identify patterns, trends, and insights
- Develop and implement predictive models and machine learning algorithms
- Design and conduct A/B tests and experiments
- Create data visualizations and dashboards for stakeholders
- Collaborate with business teams to understand requirements and deliver solutions
- Perform statistical analysis and hypothesis testing
- Build recommendation systems and forecasting models
- Communicate findings to technical and non-technical audiences
- Maintain and optimize existing data pipelines

REQUIRED SKILLS:
- Strong proficiency in Python and R programming
- Statistical analysis and hypothesis testing
- Machine Learning algorithms: regression, classification, clustering
- Data manipulation libraries: Pandas, NumPy
- Data visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, Tableau, or Power BI
- SQL and database querying (MySQL, PostgreSQL)
- Experience with Jupyter Notebooks
- Scikit-learn for model development
- Feature engineering and selection
- Model evaluation metrics and validation techniques
- Understanding of probability and statistics
- Excel and data analysis

PREFERRED SKILLS:
- Experience with big data tools (Spark, Hadoop)
- Cloud platforms: AWS (SageMaker), Azure ML, or GCP
- Time series analysis and forecasting
- Deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) experience
- A/B testing and experimental design
- Version control with Git
- Docker for containerization
- Knowledge of ETL processes
- Business intelligence tools

EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, or related field
- Certifications in data science or analytics are a plus

SOFT SKILLS:
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication and presentation abilities
- Ability to translate business problems into data solutions
- Attention to detail
- Collaborative team player
- Curiosity and continuous learning mindset

WHAT WE OFFER:
- Competitive salary package
- Performance bonuses
- Flexible work arrangements
- Professional development and training
- Health and wellness benefits
- Modern work environment
